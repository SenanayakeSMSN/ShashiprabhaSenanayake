INPUT_STREAM: SATELLITE IMAGE ACQUISITION

Ingests raw high-resolution spatial datasets and annotated GeoTIFF rasters from Google.org funding repositories. Coordinates read target geospatial grids containing potential infrastructure targets.

import rasterio
dataset = rasterio.open('wwtp_satellite_grid.tif')
print(f"Resolution: {dataset.width}x{dataset.height} | Bands: {dataset.count}")
PREPROCESSING: OPENCV OPTICAL CHAIN

Applies adaptive thresholding, Bilateral filtering noise reduction, and Canny edge algorithms to pull structural features. Isolates geometric outlines typical of aeration tanks and clarifiers from soil background noise.

gray = cv2.cvtColor(image, cv2.COLOR_BGR2GRAY)
blurred = cv2.bilateralFilter(gray, 9, 75, 75)
edges = cv2.Canny(blurred, 50, 150) # Isolating structural borders
DEEP LEARNING: YOLOv11 OBJECT INFERENCE

Processes structural spatial shapes across an optimized custom model layers array. Places target anchor bounding boxes highlighting found objects along with calculated probability weights.

model = YOLO('models/wwtp_yolov11_weights.pt')
results = model.predict(source=edges, conf=0.82)
